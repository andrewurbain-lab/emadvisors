Estate Management Advisors partners with ultra-high-net-worth individuals, families, family offices, and private households to identify exceptional private-service professionals who strengthen household operations through leadership, discretion, and service.

Our Approach

Confidentiality

Every engagement is handled with the highest level of discretion to protect the privacy of clients and candidates.

Personalized Search

Every search is tailored to the household’s unique culture, structure, expectations, and long-term goals.

Judgment Over Volume

A carefully selected group of exceptional candidates is presented rather than an overwhelming volume of résumés.

Long-Term Fit

We focus on sustainable placements that support continuity, trust, and household success.
